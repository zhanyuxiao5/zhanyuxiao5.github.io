Photography & Moving Image

Tethered:
Where the Line Holds

For Sarah Muwanga’s exhibition Tethered: Where the Line Holds at CasildART Contemporary, I created a visual record of the exhibition through photography and moving image.

The project documents the installation process, completed artworks, conversations between the artist and gallery founder, and behind-the-scenes moments from the artist interview.
