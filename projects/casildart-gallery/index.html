Tethered: Where the Line Holds

For Sarah Muwanga’s exhibition Tethered: Where the Line Holds at CasildART Contemporary, I created a visual record of the exhibition through photography and moving image.

The project documents the installation process, completed artworks, conversations between the artist and gallery founder, and behind-the-scenes moments from the artist interview.

01

Exhibition Installation

Documenting the exhibition as it developed from the installation process into its completed gallery presentation.

02

Artwork Documentation

A selection of artwork views, details and wider photographs showing the works within the gallery space.

03

In Conversation

A photographic record of a conversation between the artist and the founder of CasildART Contemporary.

04

Artist Interview

Behind-the-scenes photographs taken during an interview with the artist.
